2. GROUND TRUTH vs THE REAL MANUAL - FAILS
--------------------------------------------------------------------

This is the check that matters most, and it does not hold up.

The harvest specs and the manual specs are DISJOINT SETS. For WHB03,
the same SKU we already ingested from the real Frick manual, every
field you asked me to cross-check is simply absent:

   manual: oil charge 36 gallon (page 7)      -> harvest: ABSENT
   manual: swept volume 0.14075 ft3/rev (p4)  -> harvest: ABSENT
   manual: max speed 4306 RPM (page 4)        -> harvest: ABSENT
   manual: rotor diameter 151 mm (page 4)     -> harvest: ABSENT

What the harvest carries instead: Model, Serial No, Refrigerant,
Max Design Pressure, MAWP, MDMT, Year Built, Frick Sales Order.

Those are catalog listing and nameplate fields. The manual carries
engineering data. They describe different layers of the same machine.
So "ground truth" cannot be validated against the manual at all -
there is nothing to compare.

THE XJF VARIANT CHECK IS IMPOSSIBLE.
All four variants exist in the harvest:
   XJF-151A  -> TPC27
   XJF-151M  -> PRCA31, RWF03
   XJF-151L  -> WHB03, WHB02
   XJF-151N  -> RSI02, RSI01, JBS03
NONE of them carries a CFM field. The 342/403/500/598 distinction
cannot be cross-checked in any way.

A GENUINE CONFLICT WAS FOUND where the two sources do overlap:

   harvest WHB03 says:  "Max Speed RPM: 3600"
   our manual says:      max_speed_rpm = 4306   (070.410-IOM.pdf p4)
   divergence:           16.4 percent
   our conflict tolerance is 5 percent

Both values may be legitimate - a nameplate as-built speed versus a
manual design maximum. But merging these two sources WILL generate
conflicts that need human resolution.

A DATA ERROR IN HIS OUTPUT:
WHB03 and WHB02 are two physically different machines. They carry
the IDENTICAL serial number S0095SFMNTHAA03.

Across the whole file, 31 serial numbers are shared by more than one
record, several by four records. Examples: 33083-1 (x4),
30509-1 (x4), 27471-1 (x4), 12753996 (x4).

Serial number is the one field that must be unique per machine.

VERIFIED_SPECS ARE NOT ATOMIC.

   "Frick Bare Screw Compressor" =

That is six separate specs crammed into a single string value. It is
also where the 3600 figure is hiding. Our SpecValue expects one
atomic value per field, so this needs parsing.

ASSESSMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------

He fixed identity completely, and he was honest about the gate flag.
Both are verifiable and both are real improvements. Provenance is
present but shallower than described.

The blocker is unchanged, and v2 actually makes it easier to see:
this is catalog listing data, not verified engineering data. Naming
the array "verified_specs" implies validation against a source of
truth, but the specs were scraped from the very product page they
cite. Nothing was verified against anything external.

Where it CAN be compared against a real manual, it disagrees by
16.4 percent. It also carries 31 duplicate serial numbers, which is
a straightforward data integrity failure.

RECOMMENDATION - unchanged from v1:

Do not merge this into specs{}. Ingest it as literature-style chunks
for /search only.

That gives you 15,939 searchable catalog entries with working URLs
and clean unique ids, which is genuinely useful, without putting
unverified values next to page-cited manual values inside the
structure the FAQ engine reads.
